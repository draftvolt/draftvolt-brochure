00 Foreword

Every wall hides a decision. The question is not whether your home has wiring — but how that wiring was placed there, with what materials, and to which standard.

This catalog documents five distinct standards of electrical installation, presented in ascending order of protection, capacity and longevity. Each tier is shown through three technical drawings: a distribution panel diagram (A), a wall-section showing routing and chase work (B), and a conductor cross-section drawn at unified scale (C). Read together, the three drawings describe what the finished surface conceals.

The progression is deliberately legible. Where the lower tiers favour simplicity and minimum compliance, the upper tiers introduce progressively more layers of insulation, segmentation, surge protection, and monitoring. The choice of standard is not a matter of taste; it is a matter of the building, its load, and the horizon over which the installation is expected to serve.

Standard I of V
01
Standard I

Soviet Grade

Functional baseline. Code-essential.

Investment Grade

The minimum viable installation. Sufficient to bring power into the room and out of the socket. Materials and methods reflect the budget more than the manual; protection is reactive rather than preventive. Adequate for short-term tenancies, secondary buildings, or rooms where the cost of intervention exceeds the benefit of refinement.

  • 3–4 Circuits / Single Phase
  • Single-Pole Breakers · 1 Row
  • Rough-Chased Wall Routing
  • 1.5 mm² Single-Insulated Cu
  • Direct-Lay, No Conduit
  • 1 Year Installation Warranty
Best For: Garages, sheds, secondary structures, short-let rentals.
Standard II of V
02
Standard II

Russian Grade

Modular protection. Reinforced execution.

Investment Grade

A meaningful step into modern practice. Distribution moves to a DIN-rail enclosure with proper modular breakers and a residual current device. Cabling gains a second insulating sheath; chases are clean rather than scarred. The point at which the installation begins to behave less like a hazard and more like infrastructure.

  • 6–8 Circuits / DIN Rail
  • MCBs + Dedicated RCD (30 mA)
  • Cable Glands at Entry
  • 2.5 mm² Cu, Multi-Strand
  • Double-Sheathed PVC (ВВГнг)
  • 3 Year Installation Warranty
Best For: Apartments under renovation, rental properties at scale, light-commercial spaces.
Standard III of V
03
Standard III

European Grade

Compliant. Properly bussed. Properly buried.

Investment Grade

The standard a building inspector will sign off on without conversation. Every conductor enters a sleeve; every junction is accessible; every breaker corresponds to a labeled circuit on a printed schedule. Surge protection becomes default. Earth and neutral are buswork, not afterthoughts. The first tier intended to outlast the renovation that contains it.

  • 12–16 Circuits / RCBO Per Zone
  • Type 2 SPD / Surge Protection
  • Corrugated Conduit Throughout
  • NYM / 3-Core 2.5–6 mm² Cu
  • Dedicated N + PE Busbars
  • Circuit Schedule Printed
  • 7 Year Installation Warranty
Best For: Family homes, full apartment refits, small commercial offices, anything that should pass inspection in Berlin or Vienna.
Standard IV of V
04
Standard IV

American Grade

Two-hundred amps. Coded to NEC. Built for the long house.

Investment Grade

Service capacity steps up substantially: a 200-amp main panel with arc-fault and ground-fault protection on every habitable circuit. Cabling shifts to NM-B run through framed cavities, anchored to studs with prescribed intervals. Outlet boxes are gangable, grounded, and tamper-resistant. The North American playbook — built for larger residences, larger appliance loads, and a longer service horizon.

  • 200A Service / 40-Slot Panel
  • AFCI + GFCI on All Habitable Circuits
  • 12-2 / 14-2 NM-B (Romex)
  • Tamper-Resistant Outlets
  • Stud-Cavity Routing + Fire-Stopping
  • Dedicated Equipment Ground
  • 10 Year Installation Warranty
Best For: Larger homes, North-American-style framed construction, properties with HVAC, EV charging, induction cooking, or shop loads.
Standard V of V
05
Standard V

Premium · Smart Home

Per-circuit intelligence. Visible, measurable, addressable.

Investment Grade

A distribution system that knows itself. Each circuit is individually current-sensed and individually addressable; every breaker reports state and load to a central display and to the network. Power conductors run in shielded armor alongside dedicated low-voltage data; surge protection is integrated and self-monitoring; uninterruptible power crosses critical loads. The installation behaves less like a panel and more like a building system.

  • Intelligent Panel / LCD + App
  • Per-Circuit CT Monitoring
  • Integrated SPD + UPS Handoff
  • Armoured + Shielded Conductors
  • Parallel Cat6A / Fibre Pathways
  • Whole-House Load Analytics
  • KNX / Z-Wave / WiFi / Ethernet
  • 15 Year Installation Warranty
Best For: New construction at the high end, full smart-home retrofits, properties with solar + battery, home offices that cannot tolerate downtime, listed buildings where future re-access must be minimized.
